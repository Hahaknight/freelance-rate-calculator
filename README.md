# Freelance Rate Calculator

**What should you actually charge? Enter 5 numbers, get your floor hourly rate in 10 seconds.**

🔗 **Use it online: <https://hahaknight.github.io/freelance-rate-calculator/>**

## Why this exists

Most freelancers price by guessing what sounds tolerable — then discover at tax time that "tolerable" was below minimum wage. This calculator flips it: start from the income you *need*, subtract reality, and see the **floor rate** below which a project loses you money.

Your 5 inputs:

| Input | What it captures |
|---|---|
| Target monthly take-home | the salary you're replacing |
| Monthly business costs | software, hardware, insurance, coworking |
| Billable hours per week | realistically — not all hours are billable |
| Working weeks per year | minus holidays, sick days, dry spells |
| Estimated tax + contributions | the part everyone forgets |

Out comes the minimum defensible hourly rate. Charge above it; know when a gig is below it.

Everything runs **in your browser** — no signup, no server, your numbers never leave the page. 中文版：[freelance-rate-calculator-cn](https://github.com/Hahaknight/freelance-rate-calculator-cn).
